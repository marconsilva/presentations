MEO: Building an AI-Powered Techco — From Telco to Intelligent Platform

Conference session / Case study · ~45 min · Marco Antonio Silva, Senior Global Black Belt — Microsoft

AI Telco Digital Transformation Case Study MEO

MEO (Altice Portugal) is transforming from traditional telco to AI-powered “Techco.” This session explores AI across the entire stack — from network optimization and predictive maintenance to customer experience, content recommendation, and autonomous operations — and the platform, operating model, and cultural changes required to make that shift real.

What this session covers

How MEO can use AI as a cross-stack capability: intelligent customer journeys, predictive network operations, self-healing service management, AI-ready platform architecture, and the organizational shifts needed to move from telco efficiency to Techco leverage.
