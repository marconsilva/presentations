Very real

Multi-Agent: Hype vs. Reality

  • The hype: teams of autonomous agents replacing org charts.
  • The reality: specialized agents work best on bounded, auditable workflows.
  • Coordination design is harder than adding more agents.

“One great agent with guardrails beats five vague agents with overlapping roles.”

To shaping

Responsible AI Is Now a Product Requirement

  • Bias, hallucinations, and unsafe actions affect trust and adoption.
  • Data boundaries, security, and governance must be explicit.
  • The user experience should make confidence and limitations visible.
