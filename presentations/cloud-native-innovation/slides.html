AKS is where you go when you need Kubernetes flexibility, ecosystem depth, and platform extensibility.

  • Custom networking, service mesh, policy, and operators.
  • Multi-team platforms with opinionated internal tooling.
  • Advanced workloads including AI, GPU, and fleet scenarios.
