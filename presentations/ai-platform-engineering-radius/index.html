AI + Platform Engineering: Building Developer Platforms with Radius and Intelligent Agents

Marco Antonio Silva · Senior Global Black Belt — Microsoft

Golden paths for humans and agents Self-service infrastructure Cloud-native platform APIs
Platform Engineering Radius AI Cloud-Native Agents

Platform Engineering builds golden paths for developers. AI agents are the new consumers and builders of those paths. This session explores how Radius, Microsoft's open-source cloud-native platform, provides agent-friendly infrastructure with self-service, recipes, environments, and application-centric abstractions that help teams move from tickets and scripts to intelligent platform APIs.

What this session covers

  • Why AI changes the target user for internal developer platforms
  • How Radius maps platform engineering concepts into portable APIs and resources
  • What an agent-friendly platform needs to safely deploy and operate AI workloads
